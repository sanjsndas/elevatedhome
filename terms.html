    Terms of Privacy

    How we handle your data, our editorial commitments, and the terms that govern your use of NICHE Journal.

    Last updated: 1 March 2025

    Welcome to NICHE Journal ("NICHE", "we", "us", or "our"). These Terms of Privacy ("Terms") govern your access to and use of our website, www.nichejournal.com (the "Site"), including all content, features, and services offered on or through the Site.

    By accessing or using the Site, you agree to be bound by these Terms and our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

    Summary: We are an independent editorial publication. We don't sell your personal data. We use minimal analytics to understand what our readers value. You can opt out of cookies at any time.

    1. Who We Are

    NICHE Journal is an independent publication dedicated to thoughtful interior design, architecture, and elevated home thinking. Our editorial office is located at:

    For data-related inquiries, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@nichejournal.com.

    2. Information We Collect

    We collect information in three ways: information you provide voluntarily, information collected automatically, and information from third-party services.

    2.1 Information You Provide

    • Newsletter subscriptions: When you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address and, optionally, your first name.
    • Contact forms: If you contact us via our contact page, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
    • Comments and contributions: If you submit a comment or story pitch, we collect the information you provide.

    2.2 Information Collected Automatically

    • Usage data: We use privacy-respecting analytics (Plausible) to collect aggregated, non-identifying information such as pages visited, time on page, and referral source.
    • Device data: Browser type, operating system, and approximate geographic region (country level).
    • Cookies: We use essential cookies to remember your preferences and optional analytics cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

    2.3 Information from Third Parties

    We do not purchase or receive personal data from data brokers. If you interact with our social media accounts, those platforms may share aggregated insights with us, but never your individual identity.

    3. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    • To deliver and improve our editorial content and user experience.
    • To send you our newsletter, if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
    • To respond to your inquiries and correspondence.
    • To monitor site performance, diagnose technical issues, and prevent abuse.
    • To comply with legal obligations.

    We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

    4. Editorial Independence & Advertising

    Our editorial content is independent of advertising. We do not accept sponsored content that compromises our editorial integrity. Any sponsored or affiliate content will be clearly labeled.

    We may participate in affiliate programs, which means we may earn a commission if you purchase a product through a link on our site. This never influences our recommendations, and we only link to products we have vetted or would use ourselves.

    “We believe that trust is earned through transparency. If we ever recommend something, it's because we genuinely think it's worth your attention.”

    5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

    We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share limited information with:

    • Service providers: Trusted third parties who help us operate the Site, such as our email newsletter provider and analytics service. These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements.
    • Legal requirements: If required by law, we may disclose information to comply with a legal obligation, protect our rights, or prevent fraud.
    • Business transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before any such transfer.

    6. Data Retention

    We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in these Terms:

    • Newsletter subscription data: until you unsubscribe, plus 30 days for processing.
    • Contact form submissions: 12 months, then deleted.
    • Analytics data: aggregated and anonymized after 24 months.

    7. Your Rights

    Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
    • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
    • Deletion: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
    • Restriction: Request that we limit processing of your data.
    • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service.
    • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

    To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@nichejournal.com. We will respond within 30 days.

    8. Security

    We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    9. International Data Transfers

    NICHE is based in the United States. If you access our site from outside the US, your data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US. We comply with applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR for our European readers.

    10. Children's Privacy

    Our Site is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it.

    11. Changes to These Terms

    We may update these Terms from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced via a notice on our homepage or by email if you are a newsletter subscriber.
