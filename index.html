    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the most important principle in interior design?
    There is no single answer, but space planning — how you arrange furniture and circulation — is arguably the most fundamental. Without a good layout, even the most beautiful objects feel wrong. Light and proportion follow closely.
    How do I choose a colour palette for my home?
    Start with what you already own and love. Pull three to five colours from a favourite rug, artwork, or even a view outside. Then decide on a dominant neutral, a secondary tone, and one or two accents. Test paint samples in the actual light of the room before committing.
    Is sustainable architecture more expensive?
    Not necessarily. Passive design strategies — orientation, insulation, shading — often cost little to nothing and reduce energy bills. Some green technologies have a higher upfront cost, but many are now competitive. The long-term savings and health benefits frequently outweigh the initial investment.
    How can I make a small room feel bigger?
    Maximise natural light, use mirrors strategically, choose furniture with legs (so you can see the floor), keep sightlines clear, and opt for a cohesive colour palette. Less clutter is more effective than any trick.
