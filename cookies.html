Cookies Policy

This policy explains how NICHE Journal uses cookies and similar technologies to recognise you when you visit our site, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 15 March 2025

This Cookies Policy describes how NICHE Journal (“we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on www.nichejournal.com (the “Site”). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal data more broadly.

In short: We use cookies to keep the site running smoothly, understand how readers use our content, and remember your preferences. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be “persistent” (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or “session” cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be “first-party” (set by us) or “third-party” (set by another domain, such as an analytics provider).

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential operation: To ensure the Site functions correctly, including security, load balancing, and remembering your cookie consent preferences.
  • Analytics and performance: To understand how visitors interact with our articles, which pages are most popular, and how readers navigate the Site. This helps us improve our editorial content and user experience.
  • Functionality: To remember choices you make (such as language or region) and provide enhanced, more personalised features.
  • Marketing (limited): We do not currently run third-party advertising on NICHE Journal, but we may use cookies to measure the effectiveness of our own newsletter or social media campaigns.

The table below provides more detail about the specific cookies that may be set when you visit our Site. Please note that third-party cookies are controlled by the relevant provider and their respective privacy policies.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. The main third parties we use are:

  • Google Analytics: For anonymous traffic analysis. You can opt out via Google Analytics Opt-out.
  • Embedded content: Articles may include embedded content (e.g., YouTube videos, Instagram posts) that set their own cookies. We have no control over these.

5. How to control cookies

You can manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. The exact steps vary, so check your browser’s help section. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect how the Site works.
  • Consent tool: Where required by law, we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the “Cookie Settings” link in the footer (if available) or by clearing cookies in your browser.
  • Opt-out links: For Google Analytics, use the link above. For other third parties, please refer to their individual privacy policies.

6. Do Not Track signals

Some browsers support a “Do Not Track” (DNT) feature. Our Site does not currently respond to DNT signals, as there is no industry consensus on how to interpret them. We will continue to monitor developments in this area.

7. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will post a notice on the Site and update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
