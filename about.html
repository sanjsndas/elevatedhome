    • About NICHE

    Independent essays and practical guides on interior design, architecture, and elevated home thinking.

    NICHE is an independent publication dedicated to thoughtful interior design and architecture. We publish original essays, practical guides, and critical perspectives for people who care about how spaces shape lives.

    Founded in 2021, our mission is simple: to make good design accessible, understandable, and actionable. We believe that thoughtful spaces — whether a studio apartment or a family home — have the power to improve how we live, work, and feel.

    A passive house in Oregon, featured in our sustainable design coverage. Photo: Unsplash.

    Our Approach

    We are not a trend site. While we cover contemporary work, our focus is on enduring principles: proportion, light, material honesty, and the way people actually live in spaces. Every article is written by our editorial team or vetted contributors with professional experience in architecture and design.

    “Good design is not about luxury — it's about clarity, comfort, and a deep respect for the people who inhabit a space.”

    We avoid jargon, celebrate craft, and ask hard questions about sustainability and consumption. You won't find clickbait or endless slideshows here. Instead, you'll find long-form essays, room-by-room guides, and interviews that respect your time and intelligence.

    What We Cover

    • Interior Design — Space planning, colour theory, styling, and the details that make a room work.
    • Architecture — Residential projects, adaptive reuse, and the ideas shaping the built environment.
    • Materials — From lime plaster to reclaimed timber, we explore what things are made of and why it matters.
    • Lighting — Layered lighting strategies, fixture selection, and the science of atmosphere.
    • Sustainable Design — Passive strategies, low-carbon materials, and homes that tread lightly.
    • Modern Homes — Tours and analysis of contemporary living spaces around the world.

    Editorial Independence

    All articles are written by our editorial team. We may occasionally use affiliate links; these never influence our recommendations. We are committed to transparency and accuracy. We do not accept sponsored content that compromises our editorial integrity.
