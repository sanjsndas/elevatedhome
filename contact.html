Frequently asked questions

How do I pitch a story to NICHE?
Send a short pitch (200–300 words) to editors@nichejournal.com with the subject line “Pitch: [topic]”. Include a brief outline, why it fits our audience, and any relevant links or credentials. We accept pitches from both established and emerging writers.
Do you accept guest posts?
We occasionally publish guest essays from architects, designers, and researchers. Please pitch first — we don't accept unsolicited full drafts. All guest pieces are edited in-house and must be original, unpublished work.
Can I use your images or republish an article?
Unless otherwise noted, all content is copyrighted. For republishing or syndication requests, please contact editors@nichejournal.com. We're sometimes able to grant permission with proper attribution and a link back.
I have a product or project I'd like you to feature. How do I submit it?
We're always looking for interesting projects. Send a concise description, high-resolution images, and a link to your portfolio or project page to editors@nichejournal.com. Due to volume, we can't respond to every submission, but we do review all of them.
How do I contact you about a correction?
Accuracy matters to us. Email editors@nichejournal.com with the article title, the specific error, and any supporting evidence. We'll review and issue a correction promptly if needed.
